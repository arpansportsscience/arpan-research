From training load to a measurable signal

Mitochondria release fragments of their own DNA into the bloodstream when cells are stressed or damaged. Measuring those fragments gives a window into how the body copes with training.

Key terms

What is cell-free mitochondrial DNA?

Short pieces of mitochondrial DNA that circulate in blood plasma outside of cells. They are released during cellular stress, damage or active secretion.

Why study it in athletes?

Athletes repeatedly push their physiology near its limits. A blood marker that responds to that load could help separate productive adaptation from excessive strain.

How is it different from other biomarkers?

Common markers reflect muscle damage or metabolism. Mitochondrial DNA reflects stress at the organelle that powers the cell, which may respond on a different timescale.
